83 | 1. How do I discover and book a vendor on Magizhnaazh?
84 | Browse our Marketplace tab by selecting your city (e.g. Coimbatore, Chennai, Madurai) and category (Catering, Venues, Decor, Photography, etc.). 85 | 2. How do advance payments work?
86 | When booking a vendor, you submit an advance payment claim. Magizhnaazh holds this in your booking ledger until the vendor verifies availability and confirms the booking. You can monitor the real-time status under <strong>My Orders</strong>.
87 | 3. How does the Smart Budget Planner work?
88 | Our Smart Budget Planner automatically divides your total target budget across essential categories (Venues, Catering, Stage Decor, Photography, Makeup, Gifts). Whenever you confirm a vendor booking, the cost is automatically deducted from your remaining allocation.
89 | 4. Can I customize digital invitations?
