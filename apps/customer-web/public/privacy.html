Privacy Policy

Magizhnaazh, operated by Porulon Technologies · Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Porulon Technologies ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Magizhnaazh event-planning marketplace ("the Service") at event-customer.porulontech.com and its related vendor and administration sites.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you register or sign in (including via Google Sign-In, which shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us).
  • Contact details — phone number and city, when you choose to provide them.
  • Event and booking details — event type, date, guest count, budget, and the vendor bookings and messages you create on the Service.
  • Technical data — basic device and log information needed to operate and secure the Service.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To provide the core marketplace features — creating events, discovering vendors, and making bookings.
  • To connect you with vendors you choose to contact or book.
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

How we share information

We share your information only as needed to provide the Service — for example, sharing your booking and contact details with a vendor you book. We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as email delivery), and where required by law.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) solely to create and identify your account. We do not use this data for advertising and do not share it with third parties except as described above. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us.

Your choices

You can access or update your account details within the app, and you can request correction or deletion of your personal data by emailing us.

Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at porulontechnologies@gmail.com.
