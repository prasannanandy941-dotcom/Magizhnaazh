How Can We Help You?

Quick answers to common questions about booking vendors, managing event budgets, and digital invitations.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I discover and book a vendor on Magizhnaazh?

Browse our Marketplace tab by selecting your city (e.g. Coimbatore, Chennai, Madurai) and category (Catering, Venues, Decor, Photography, etc.). Click View Details to review portfolios, pricing packages, and past ratings. Once selected, click Book Vendor to request your booking date.

2. How do advance payments work?

When booking a vendor, you submit an advance payment claim. Magizhnaazh holds this in your booking ledger until the vendor verifies availability and confirms the booking. You can monitor the real-time status under My Orders.

3. How does the Smart Budget Planner work?

Our Smart Budget Planner automatically divides your total target budget across essential categories (Venues, Catering, Stage Decor, Photography, Makeup, Gifts). Whenever you confirm a vendor booking, the cost is automatically deducted from your remaining allocation.

4. Can I customize digital invitations?

Yes! Our Canva-style invitation designer allows you to customize typography, venue details, and generate a dynamic QR code that your guests can scan to submit their RSVP and food preferences online.

5. What happens if a vendor is unavailable on my date?

If a vendor cannot fulfill your date, the request is marked cancelled and your advance payment is promptly refunded or credited back according to our transparent policy.
