Returns & Refunds Policy

Clear, fair, and transparent booking cancellation and advance payment terms operated by Porulon Technologies.

1. Advance Payment Guarantee

When you book a vendor service on Magizhnaazh (such as Mandapams, Feast Catering, Stage Decoration, or Photography), you submit an advance deposit to secure your event date.

Your advance payment remains under customer protection until the vendor officially confirms calendar availability. If a vendor is unavailable or unable to confirm your booking date, 100% of your advance payment is promptly refunded.

2. Cancellation Timelines

We recognize that family event schedules and dates occasionally need to change. Cancellation policies depend on the notice period before the scheduled event date:

  • Cancellation more than 30 days prior: Eligible for full refund of the platform advance deposit, minus nominal processing fees.
  • Cancellation between 15 to 30 days prior: 50% refund of the advance deposit, or option to reschedule to an alternative available date with the same vendor without penalty.
  • Cancellation less than 15 days prior: The advance deposit is retained by the vendor to cover procurement, reserved dates, and perishable preparations (e.g. food raw materials, floral orders).

3. Quality & Service Discrepancy

If an event vendor fails to deliver the promised package, arrives significantly late, or cancels on the day of the event, our dedicated dispute resolution desk will immediately step in. In verified cases of vendor default, you are entitled to a full refund and priority assistance finding replacement vendors.
