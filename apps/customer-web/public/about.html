About Us

Crafting Unforgettable Celebrations

Tamil Nadu's premier event planning & vendor marketplace, connecting families with the finest artisans, venues, and caterers.

Who We Are

Magizhnaazh is an integrated celebration and event planning technology platform operated by Porulon Technologies Private Limited, headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

Planning a wedding, birthday, housewarming, or milestone ceremony is a sacred and joy-filled experience. However, coordinating across multiple vendors, negotiating rates, managing guestlists, and tracking budgets often creates needless stress. We built Magizhnaazh to replace that chaos with beauty, clarity, and peace of mind.

What We Offer

🏛️ Verified Venues & Mandapams

Carefully vetted marriage halls, luxury banquet ballrooms, open lawns, and heritage spaces across Coimbatore, Chennai, Madurai, and beyond.

🍛 Authentic Feast Catering

From traditional South Indian banana leaf wedding feasts to multi-cuisine cocktail receptions with transparent per-plate pricing.

📸 Photography & Cinema

Award-winning candid wedding storytellers, traditional photographers, and cinematic filmmakers ready to immortalize your special moments.

🌸 Bespoke Floral & Stage Decor

Master decorators specializing in fresh flower mandapams, elegant reception stages, lighting, and customized thematic backdrops.

💰 Smart Budget Planner

AI-driven event budget allocation tools ensuring you stay right on target without unexpected surprises.

💌 Digital RSVP Invitations

Custom Canva-style digital invites with real-time guest RSVP tracking and dietary preference collection.
